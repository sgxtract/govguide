# Lesson 04 — Service guide page

**Built:** guide page with at-a-glance facts, requirements checklist, timetable steps, fee table with total, office details (hours, tel: links, map), online options, tips, FAQs (`<details>`), related guides, sources, "Report outdated info", draft and "may be outdated" notices. Formatting helpers (`Intl`, en-PH) and `VerifyText`.

**Remember:**

- In dev, yellow `[VERIFY]` marks show exactly what's left to check.
- Drafts aren't built in production; their URLs 404.
- The stale notice is computed at build time, so a weekly rebuild keeps it honest (set up in Lesson 12).
