# Lesson 05 — Agency & office pages

**Built:** offices index, agency page (official channels, offices with hours, guides), links from guides and city home. Repository: `listCityAgencies`, and unverified agencies/offices are hidden in production.

**Remember:**

- An agency gets a city page only if it has a verified office or a visible guide there.
- Link to one office with `/[city]/agencies/[agency]#[office-slug]`.
- Agencies and offices go public only when they have zero `[VERIFY]`.
